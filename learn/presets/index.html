How to use Presets in Flashloop

A preset is a saved recipe for one style of video, like Sports Anime or Claymation. Add one to a Super Agent chat, say what should happen, and the agent plans and makes the clips in that style.

Five things to put in one message

The preset already knows the look, so your message only needs the story. Put these five things in it and the agent rarely has to ask anything.

PartWhat to writeExample
PresetAttach it, or name it in the messageSports Anime
What happensOne clear moment or story beatA rookie scores in the 94th minute
WhoDescribe them, @ a saved character, or attach a photoKaito, 17, number 11, red boots
How long15, 30 or 60 seconds, or a number of scenes30 seconds

Ask first, or let it run

Choose how much the agent checks with you before it spends credits.

ModeWhat it doesPick it when
Ask modeAsks before big steps and shows the cost before it generatesYou want to check the plan and the cost first
Generate without askingRuns the whole job on its own and picks sensible defaultsYour first message already covers everything

Every step uses credits

Planning and every clip use credits from your balance. Each clip costs that model's normal price.

You see the cost first

In Ask mode the agent shows the cost and waits for your OK. Ask for shorter clips or a 480p draft to spend less.

If it stops

Your chat keeps everything above the stop card. Top up or change your plan, then type "continue".

Skills work on any paid plan: Starter, Creator or Ultra. Compare plans

Make a preset of your own

Save a look you like as a preset and use it in any chat.

Ask the agent to write one

After a video you like, say "turn this into a preset". The agent writes the recipe and you press Save preset. Works on the web and in the app.

Write one yourself

Open Presets and press New preset. Give it a short name and describe the look, the shots and the voice. Editing is on the website.

Import a folder

Drop a folder, a .zip or a .skill file on the Presets page. Any folder with a SKILL.md becomes a preset.

Share it

Share gives you a link. Anyone signed in can open it and clone the preset into their own list.

When something goes wrong

What you seeWhyFix
It stopped right after "Loaded preset"Your credits ran out on the first step. Reading a preset and planning use credits before any clip is made.Top up or pick a plan, then type "continue". Your chat keeps everything so far.
It keeps asking me questionsYour message left out the length, the format or the story.Answer in one message, or put those three things in your first message next time.
It used a different style than I wantedNo preset was attached, so the agent picked the closest one.Attach the preset with + or / so it is always used.
A clip failedMost often not enough credits, a photo that had not finished uploading, or reference videos longer than 15 seconds in total.Check your balance, wait for uploads to finish, or trim the reference video.
