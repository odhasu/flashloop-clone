Flashloop MCP

Ask Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor for a video, an image, a character or a voiceover. Flashloop makes it right inside your chat.

1

Grab your server link

This is Flashloop's MCP address. Tap it to copy, then keep it handy.

2

Add it to Claude

Head to Claude's connector settings, create a new connector called Flashloop and drop the link in.

Open Connectors
3

Approve and start creating

Sign in with your Flashloop account when Claude asks to connect. That's it, your first video is one prompt away.

Got any questions left?

We've answered the most frequently asked questions.

A bridge between your AI assistant and Flashloop, built on the open Model Context Protocol. Once connected, your assistant can create videos, images, characters and voiceovers with your Flashloop account. You describe what you want, it handles the rest, and you never leave the conversation.

Adding the connector costs nothing. When your assistant generates something, it spends credits from your regular Flashloop plan. It is the same balance you use on the site, at the same prices.

No keys, no tokens, nothing to paste from a dashboard. The first time your assistant connects, a Flashloop sign-in window opens in your browser. Approve it once and the link stays active.

Anything you'd make in Flashloop itself: clips on Veo 3.1, Sora 2, Kling 3.0 or Seedance 2.0, stills with Nano Banana Pro or GPT Image 2, reusable characters that stay consistent across scenes, plus voiceovers, voice cloning and audio translation.

Everything lands in your Flashloop library, exactly like creations made on the site. Download them straight from the chat, or open Flashloop later to edit, animate or reuse them.

Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor all work today — pick your client above and follow its three steps. Under the hood it is one server with one URL, so any other MCP-compatible assistant (Claude Code, Windsurf, Goose and friends) connects with the same address too.
