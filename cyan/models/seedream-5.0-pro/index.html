What is Seedream 5.0 Pro?

Seedream 5.0 Pro is ByteDance's multimodal image model for controlled generation, precision editing and complex information visualization, such as infographics, diagrams and posters.

It also produces realistic portraits and renders text in multiple languages. On Flashloop it takes a text prompt or up to 10 reference images and outputs at 1K in Basic quality or 2K in High quality.

The model is aimed at work where the layout has to be right: labeled diagrams, posters with several blocks of copy, menus and packaging. Prompts can run to about 5,000 characters, which leaves room to specify every element, its wording and its position. References let you steer identity, style or composition instead of describing them.

Seedream is ByteDance's image line and is separate from Seedance, its video models. Pick Seedream 5.0 Pro for text-heavy and information-heavy images and for precise edits. Seedream 5.0 Lite is cheaper and faster and goes up to 3K. Nano Banana Pro is worth comparing for plain-English photo edits.

Where Seedream 5.0 Pro fits best

  • Infographics and diagrams

    Describe each element and its label and get a structured graphic with readable text, such as a water cycle or a product anatomy diagram.

  • Menus, signs and posters

    Write the exact wording in quotes and place it by section for hand-lettered boards, event posters and storefront signs.

  • Realistic portraits

    Generate studio-style portraits at 2K, with references to guide the subject or the look.

  • Precise edits from references

    Attach up to 10 images and say what each one controls to change one thing while keeping the rest.

  • Cinematic scenes

    Use ultrawide 21:9 or 16:9 at 2K for landscapes and establishing shots.

Questions about Seedream 5.0 Pro

What is the difference between Basic and High quality in Seedream 5.0 Pro?

Basic renders at 1K and High renders at 2K. Basic is cheaper and works well for drafts, and High is the tier to use for final images with fine detail or small text.

How much does Seedream 5.0 Pro cost on Flashloop?

650 credits per image at Basic (1K) and 1,250 credits at High (2K), plus 50 credits for each reference image you attach.

Is Seedream 5.0 Pro good at rendering text?

Yes. ByteDance built it for multilingual text and information-heavy layouts such as infographics, diagrams and posters, so it handles menus, signs and labeled graphics well.

How many reference images can I use?

Up to 10. Use them to control identity, style or layout, or to make a precise edit to an existing image.

What aspect ratios does Seedream 5.0 Pro support?

1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16, 2:3, 3:2 and 21:9, which covers social posts, posters, banners and ultrawide scenes.

Should I use Seedream 5.0 Pro or Seedream 5.0 Lite?

Choose Pro for infographics, precise edits, realistic portraits and layouts with lots of text. Choose Lite for quick, cheaper images or when you need 3K output.

Who makes Seedream 5.0 Pro?

ByteDance.

Is Seedream the same as Seedance?

No. Both come from ByteDance, but Seedream is the image model family and Seedance is the video family.
