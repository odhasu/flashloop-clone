7 Best Leonardo.Ai Alternatives in 2026 (Battle Tested)

By Victor Saisse
Founding Engineer at Flashloop

Leonardo.Ai is still an image maker's playground, but video is tugging at its sleeve. This list starts with that fork: double down on images, or move toward motion without losing your mind.

Leonardo.Ai is still easiest to understand as an image studio, albeit one with a rather large video wing. The Canva-owned platform runs on web, iOS and Android. It trains its own Lucid Origin, Lucid Realism and Phoenix image models, lets you train LoRAs called Elements on your own images, and lists 34 video models, including Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 and its own Motion 2.0. There is a permanent free plan. Paid plans start at $12 a month.

That breadth is exactly why a generic replacement list is not much use. If you live in Elements, Realtime Canvas or Canva, leaving could make your workflow worse. If you mostly open Leonardo.Ai for Seedance or Kling, the decision is different. Reviewers also raise prompt filters, public free-plan outputs and tokens that disappear when a subscription ends. Flagship video models always cost tokens, even on Premium and Ultimate. Since Flashloop and Leonardo.Ai share much of the same video shelf, this comparison is about the surrounding workflow and the bill.

The catches behind the generous free plan

Leonardo.Ai's Essential plan costs $12 a month, or $10 a month billed yearly, for 8,500 tokens. The public complaints worth reading are less about that headline price and more about what happens to prompts, privacy and unused tokens.

What Leonardo.Ai users complain about
  • Some reviewers say content filters block harmless prompts and don't explain what triggered the block. Leonardo has replied that its filter is mandatory and can't be turned off, and its FAQ says free accounts get stricter filtering. (Sources: Trustpilot summary, Trustpilot 1, Trustpilot 2, Trustpilot 3, Leonardo FAQ)
  • Some reviewers report charges after cancelling and refunds that were hard to get. Leonardo's terms say a cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period, and its refund policy excludes change of mind. (Sources: Trustpilot 1, Trustpilot 2, Trustpilot 3, Refund policy, Terms of Service)
  • App Store and Trustpilot reviewers say saved tokens vanish when a subscription ends. That matches Leonardo's rules: the rollover bank is cancelled when you cancel, and top-up tokens only work while you're subscribed. (Sources: App Store reviews, App Store reviews 2, Trustpilot 1, Token Rollover, Top-up Tokens)
  • Free users complain that their creations are public and can't be deleted unless they upgrade. Leonardo's help center confirms that Private Mode and deleting generated images both require a paid plan. (Sources: Trustpilot 1, Trustpilot 2, Google Play reviews, Leonardo FAQ, Private & Public Images)
  • Some users say results ignore the prompt, so tokens go on images and videos they can't use. Trustpilot's summary of user experience also mentions video generation failing to follow prompts. (Sources: Trustpilot summary, Trustpilot 1, Trustpilot 2, Trustpilot 3, App Store reviews)

This is not a claim that every Leonardo.Ai customer is miserable. Its Trustpilot score is 4.6 from 1,465 reviews, the iOS app holds 4.83 stars from 10,835 US ratings, and the Android app has 10M+ downloads with a 4.7 rating. Paid plans can bank unused tokens for up to three months. Support assesses refunds under Australian Consumer Law, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period. Flashloop is not saintly on this point either: its terms make purchases final and non-refundable, and plan credits do not roll over.

What we checked, and what we did not

A disclosure before any ranking: we build Flashloop. We know its rough edges as well as its model picker, and we use models inside it every day, including Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 and Wan 3.0.

We did not pretend to run a seven-platform lab test. For Leonardo.Ai and the other six tools, we checked live pricing, model lists, App Store and Google Play listings, and public reviews in September 2026. Their sections therefore show homepages, not clips we claim to have made. The quick summaries stick to details a buyer can verify: video models, free access, entry price, mobile apps and the largest limitation we found.

Choose Flashloop when video has become the main job

Flashloop makes the stronger case when video, voice and repeatable formats matter more than image training. These are the questions that point in its direction:

  • “Can I make this viral format with my own character?”
  • “Can I clone my voice or dub this clip into another language?”
  • “Can I generate video from ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor?”
  • “Can I keep every model in one library without public free-plan outputs?”

Keep Leonardo.Ai when the image workflow is the point

Leonardo.Ai is stronger if you train image models. Elements are LoRAs trained on your own images, with 10, 20 or 50 personal models a month on Essential, Premium and Ultimate. It has a permanent free plan with 150 tokens a day, and Essential is $12 a month. Premium keeps generating images in unlimited relaxed mode on Leonardo-hosted models such as Lucid Origin, Phoenix and FLUX Dev after tokens run out. Ultimate adds relaxed video on Motion 2.0. Realtime Canvas turns sketches into images as you draw. It also makes 3D models with Rodin V2, and individual users can send an image to Canva in one click. If that image-training loop, the free plan or the Canva handoff is why you use Leonardo.Ai, staying there makes sense.

My verdict

If Leonardo.Ai has become an expensive launcher for Seedance or Kling, Flashloop is worth opening next for trend workflows, voice cloning and a video-first library. You keep those model families. You do not keep LoRA training, Realtime Canvas, 3D generation, Canva handoff or a free plan.

If LoRAs, the free plan or Canva are the point, my advice is less exciting and probably cheaper: stay with Leonardo.Ai.

OpenArt fits creators who train characters and worlds before animating them. If you want a free plan and a phone app, Leonardo.Ai still covers that better. Video-first creators should look at Flashloop or Krea first.

Do the token math with your own prompts

The monthly price is the cover charge. The useful number is what your normal clip costs after retries, at your usual duration and resolution. Four checks matter for a Leonardo.Ai move:

  1. Price one finished clip. On Leonardo.Ai, Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0 and Veo 3.1 always cost tokens and are excluded from unlimited relaxed generation, even on Premium and Ultimate.
  2. Match the plan to the model. Veo on Leonardo.Ai is paid-only. On Flashloop, Kling 3.0 and Seedance 2.5 start on Creator, not Starter. On Pixlr, all video models start on Premium, not Plus.
  3. Read the yearly total. Leonardo.Ai shows monthly by default, with yearly billed at $10 / $24 / $48 a month. ImagineArt opens on yearly. There is no first-month promo on Leonardo.Ai's public pricing page.
  4. Count the workflow you would have to rebuild. Elements (LoRAs) and Canva handoff do not move with you. If you only needed another video model, one multi-model plan can cost less than a second subscription, but you still have to rebuild trained image models.

Leonardo.Ai's pricing and promotions change, so check its current pricing page before deciding.

Do you need to leave Leonardo.Ai?

My short answer is no if Elements, Realtime Canvas, 3D or Canva are in your weekly routine. Leonardo.Ai remains the stronger image-training studio in this comparison, and it is cheaper to start because it has a real free plan. Flashloop is our pick for the narrower case where video has taken over: you keep Seedance, Kling, Veo and Wan, then add trend Apps, voice cloning and dubbing without public free-plan outputs.

The rest of the shortlist depends on what you are trying to preserve:

  • Choose Flashloop if you want video-first models, trend workflows and a phone app, without public free-plan outputs.
  • Choose Krea if you want to experiment, compare models and build node workflows.
  • Choose OpenArt if you train characters and worlds before video.
  • Choose ImagineArt if you want a large app library at a similar monthly price.
  • Choose Pixlr if you need a classic photo editor and AI video is extra.
  • Choose Magnific if you need stock, design tools and AI in one stack.
  • Choose Runway if you're making professional cinematic work or need studio formats.

If you train Elements, rely on the 150-token free plan, use Realtime Canvas or send work to Canva, keep Leonardo.Ai until a replacement can cover that exact job. A shiny model grid is not compensation for losing the part of the product you use every day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Leonardo.Ai alternative?

If video has become the main job, our pick is Flashloop. It puts Seedance, Kling, Veo and Wan inside a video-first app with trend workflows, voice cloning, dubbing, iOS and Android. Leonardo.Ai already carries many of the same models, so this is a workflow decision, not a treasure hunt for a missing engine.

Krea is a strong alternative if you want a node canvas, and OpenArt if you train characters and worlds before video.

Is Flashloop better than Leonardo.Ai?

It depends on the workflow.

Flashloop is a better fit if you want ready-made trend Apps, reusable characters, voice cloning and dubbing, and video generation through MCP. Leonardo.Ai is the better fit if you train LoRAs (Elements), want its own Lucid and Phoenix image models, need a permanent free plan, or send images into Canva. Flashloop has no LoRA training and no free plan. Flagship video models such as Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0 and Veo 3.1 still cost tokens on every Leonardo.Ai plan, including Premium and Ultimate.

What is the best free Leonardo.Ai alternative?

Krea's free plan gives you 100 compute units a day with no credit card, though free video is limited to 2 LTX-2 clips a day. Pixlr can be used without paying, with ads, 3 saves a day and 20 AI credits for new accounts. ImagineArt gives 100 credits a day, but video generation needs a paid plan. Runway gives 125 one-time credits with watermarked videos. Flashloop has no free plan.

Leonardo.Ai itself has a permanent free plan with 150 tokens a day. Those creations are public, free videos are watermarked, and not every video model is open to free users. Free tiers change often, so check the current limits before choosing a platform around them.

Which Leonardo.Ai alternative is cheaper?

There isn't a universal answer. Leonardo.Ai's cheapest paid plan is Essential at $12 a month, or $10 a month billed yearly. Among the alternatives, Pixlr Plus starts at $2.49 a month, but access to all image and video models starts on Premium at $9.99 a month. Krea Basic is $9 a month, ImagineArt Basic is $13 a month, OpenArt Starter is $14 a month, Runway Standard is $15 a month and Flashloop Starter is $19 a month.

Cheaper plans often leave out the newest models. On Flashloop, Seedance 2.5 and Kling 3.0 start on Creator at $59 a month, or $47 a month billed annually. On Leonardo.Ai those models are listed from Essential, but they always cost tokens and never run in unlimited relaxed mode.

Does Leonardo.Ai have a mobile app?

Yes. Leonardo.Ai has official iOS and Android apps, both listed as Leonardo.Ai - Image Generator. There is no official Mac or Windows desktop app. Among the alternatives, Flashloop, ImagineArt, Pixlr, Magnific and Runway have iOS and Android apps, Krea has an iOS app, and OpenArt has no official app.

Can I train my own image models on Flashloop?

No. Flashloop does not offer personal model or LoRA training. Leonardo.Ai's Elements feature trains LoRAs on your own images, with 10, 20 or 50 personal models a month on Essential, Premium and Ultimate. If that training loop is why you use Leonardo.Ai, OpenArt is the closer alternative on this list: even its Starter plan includes about 13 personalized model trainings a month.

Do I still get Seedance, Kling and Veo if I leave Leonardo.Ai?

Yes, on several of these platforms. Flashloop, Krea, OpenArt, ImagineArt, Magnific and Runway all list Seedance 2.5 and Kling 3.0. Flashloop, Leonardo.Ai, Krea, OpenArt, ImagineArt, Magnific and Runway list Veo 3.1. Pixlr lists Veo Fast 3.1 and Veo Lite 3.1.

Sora 2 is not a reason to stay or switch for Flashloop: Flashloop does not offer Sora, and Leonardo.Ai removed Sora 2 and Sora 2 Pro in July 2026 after a provider-side change by OpenAI. Its pricing FAQ still naming Sora 2 is out of date.
