A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we
press play.

Something else on your mind?
Put it in your brief

Do I need to film anything?

For fully animated projects, you do not need to be on camera. We can work from your idea and visual references. If your project needs product images, existing footage, or brand assets, we will agree on those in the brief.

What can we create together?

Character-led stories, animated shorts, creator content, product stories, and AI-assisted campaigns. Share the style you have in mind, even if it is still an early idea, and we can work out the right approach.

How much does a project cost?

Studio work is quoted for the project. The number of videos, complexity, length, and production needs all affect the scope. Include an estimated budget in your brief if you have one; pricing is agreed before production starts.

How long does production take?

Timing depends on the creative direction, number of videos, and feedback rounds. Tell us about any deadline in your brief so we can discuss a realistic schedule before you commit.

What happens after I create my brief?

You can review, edit, and copy your brief here. This preview does not send an inquiry or upload your details. When you are happy with it, contact @nateassets on Instagram and share the copied brief to start a conversation.

Can I use the finished videos commercially?

Usage rights, deliverables, and any restrictions are agreed in the project terms before production. Let us know where you plan to publish or advertise so the scope can cover your intended use.
