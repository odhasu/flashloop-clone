How to choose a model

  • Native dialogue or sound in the render

    Veo 3.1, Kling 4.0, Kling 2.6, and 12 more

  • The longest clips, up to 30 seconds

    Kling 4.0, Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0, and 2 more

  • The most aspect ratio options for one video model

    Kling 4.0, Seedance 2.5, Seedance 2.0, and 8 more

  • High-resolution stills for print or a large display

    Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2.1, Seedream 5.0 Lite, and 1 more

  • Enlarging a file you already generated

    Topaz Image Upscale or Topaz Video Upscale, both work directly from My Assets

Questions about AI models on Flashloop

How many AI models does Flashloop support?

Flashloop currently runs 27 AI models: 18 for video, 7 for image, and 2 for upscaling, all available on iOS, Android, and web.

Which AI video model should I use?

It depends on what you need. Veo 3.1 is built for native audio, Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0, Wan 3.0 Prime and Grok Imagine render the longest clips, up to 30 seconds, and the Seedance 2.0 family, H3 Max and Hailuo 3 offer the most aspect ratios. Each model page includes a full comparison with related models.

Which AI image model should I use?

Nano Banana Pro is built for natural language editing and multilingual text, Seedream 5.0 Lite reaches 4K fast, Flux 2 trades top resolution for speed tiers, and GPT Image 2 and GPT Image 2.5 bring OpenAI's rendering style.

Can I upscale a video or image I already generated on Flashloop?

Yes. Topaz Image Upscale and Topaz Video Upscale both accept files from My Assets, so you can enlarge anything created on Flashloop without re-uploading it.
