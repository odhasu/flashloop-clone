Questions about the image editor

Does the rest of the image change when I edit one area?

No. With Regional edit or Lasso edit, the pixels outside your selection stay exactly as they were.

Which selection tools can I use?

Regional edit selects a rectangle and Lasso edit selects a freehand shape. You can also describe an edit for the whole image with no selection, and add a reference image from an upload or your assets.

Can I expand an image to 16:9?

Yes. Expand and Crop offers Free, 1:1, 16:9, 9:16, 4:3 and 3:4 frames. Extending past the edges is an AI outpaint.

Is cropping free?

Cropping inside the image is free. Extending past the edges is an AI outpaint and costs credits. Each Generate button shows its credit cost before you run it.

Can I remove the background from an image?

Yes. Remove background works on the selected layer or on the whole image.

Which AI model does the image editor use?

Edits run on GPT Image 2.5.

What can I download?

You can download your layers as a .zip, the original image, or the edited image as a .png.

Can I keep editing later?

Yes. Projects autosave, and when you are signed in your projects are listed at the top of this page so you can reopen them.

Can I send the result back to the image generator?

Yes. Use as reference sends your result to the image generator. Upscale hands the image off to the upscaler.
