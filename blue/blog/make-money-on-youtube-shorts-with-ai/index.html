1

Step 1: Pick your niche

Go to YouTube and search the type of content you'd like to make

Search the type of content you'd like to make
  • Kids stories
  • Cute animals
  • Cartoons
  • Satisfying videos
  • Fun facts
  • Short dramas
  • History stories...

Pick something you'd actually enjoy making 30 videos about. You'll be living in this niche for a while

Find 3 to 5 channels in that niche that are blowing up

Room to grow

Channel created

Less than a year ago

Look at when the channel was created (About tab). If it's less than a year old and already has big numbers, that's proof the niche still has room

Skip it

Channel created

10 years of history

Avoid niches where only giant channels with 10 years of history win

Open their Shorts tab and sort by "Popular"

  • Note the top 10 videos of each channel in a doc or your notes app

For each one, write down: the hook (what happens in the first 2 seconds), the main character, the topic, and the length

Look for the pattern

  • Same recurring character?
  • Same type of story?
  • Same visual style?
That pattern is your starting recipe
2

Step 2: Do the math

Look at how many views these channels get per month

Add up the views on their Shorts from the last 30 days, or use a free tool like Social Blade to see their monthly views

Look up how much YouTube pays per 1M Shorts views in that niche

Search YouTube Shorts RPM [your niche]

RPM changes a lot from one niche to another, so don't trust a single number. Check a few sources

Multiply. That's what's on the table for you

million

Fill in both numbers above

Don't skip this step

Seeing the number is what keeps you posting on the days you don't feel like it

Be realistic with your timeline

Most channels take a few weeks of daily posting before one video takes off. Plan for it

3

Step 3: Set up your channel

Create a new YouTube channel dedicated to your niche

  • Use a brand account (not your personal name) so you can add people later if you grow

One channel = one niche

Don't mix kids cartoons with finance tips, the algorithm won't know who to show you to

Pick a simple, memorable name

  • Easy to spell
  • Easy to search
  • Tells people what they'll get

Create your profile picture and banner in Flashloop

Go to Flashloop, open Image, and describe your channel's vibe

Even better

If your channel has a main character, create it first (see Step 6) and use it as your profile picture. People subscribe to characters

Write a short channel description

2 lines: what your channel is about and how often you post ("New story every day")

4

Step 4: Get your video ideas

Open the Flashloop Super Agent at flashloop.ai/agent (or ChatGPT / Claude if you prefer)

Open the Super Agent

Ask for 20 viral YouTube Shorts ideas for your niche

  • Paste the top videos you found in Step 1 and explain what they have in common. The more examples you give, the better the ideas

Pick the one you like most

Choose the idea with the strongest hook, not the one with the best story. On Shorts, the first 2 seconds decide everything

Save the other 19

That's your content calendar for the next 3 weeks

5

Step 5: Turn the idea into a script

Ask for a scene by scene breakdown for a 1 minute YouTube Short

Check the breakdown before moving on

  • Is the hook strong?
  • Does every scene move the story forward?
  • Is there a payoff at the end?

Ask for changes until you're happy. Fixing the script is free, fixing the video costs time

Keep it to 6 to 10 scenes

Short scenes = fast pacing = people keep watching

6

Step 6: Make the video with the Flashloop Super Agent

This is where everything comes together. Instead of jumping between an image tool, a video tool, a voice tool and an editing app, the Super Agent does it all in one place.

Go to flashloop.ai/agent

Create your main character first (only once)

  • Use Flashloop's Characters feature so your character looks the same in every scene and every video
This is the secret of the big channels: a recognizable character people come back for

Paste your scene by scene breakdown into the agent

Let it cook 🍳

  1. The agent picks the best AI model for each scene (Veo, Kling, Seedance and more), so you don't have to know which one to use

    VeoKlingSeedanceand more

  2. It shows you the cost in credits before generating, so there are no surprises

    Cost in creditsApprove

  3. It handles the scenes, the voice and puts everything together

Review the result and ask for changes in plain English

  • Make scene 3 more dramatic
  • The voice should sound younger
  • Add a close up on the character's face in scene 1

No timeline, no editing software. Just talk to it

Upscale if needed and download your video

Use Upscale for a sharper final result before posting

7

Step 7: Post and repeat

Upload your video as a YouTube Short

Title

under 60 characters

Short, curious, makes people want to know what happens

Description

#shorts#yourniche

1 line about the video + 3 to 5 relevant hashtags (#shorts + your niche)

Audience

Yes, it's made for kidsNo, it's not made for kids

Set it correctly. If it's made for kids, select "Made for kids" (required by YouTube)

Post every day if you can

  • Same time every day helps. Your viewers get used to it
  • Batch it: make 3 to 5 videos in one session with the agent, then schedule them in YouTube Studio

Every week, check your analytics in YouTube Studio

Look at 2 numbers: views and viewed vs swiped away.

Views

Viewed vs swiped away

ViewedSwiped away

Fix the hook

If most people swipe away, your hook needs work

Double down on what works

  • Take your top 3 videos of the week, give them to the agent, and ask for 10 new ideas in the same style
  • Keep your character, keep your style, change the story every time
