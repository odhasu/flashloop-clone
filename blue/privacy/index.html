1. Introduction

Flashloop ("Flashloop", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use the Flashloop website, mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using Flashloop, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect several types of information in connection with your use of the Service.

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Username or profile information
  • Account credentials

2.2 Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party providers such as Stripe or mobile app stores.

Flashloop does not store full payment card numbers.

Payment providers may collect billing details necessary to process transactions.

2.3 Usage Information

We may automatically collect information about how the Service is used, including:

  • IP address
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Browser type
  • App version
  • Interaction with platform features
  • Generation requests and activity logs

2.4 Content Data

To operate the Service, we may process:

  • Prompts submitted to AI models
  • Uploaded media files
  • Generated images or videos
  • Prompts, files and settings that an AI assistant you connect sends to Flashloop (see the AI Assistant Connections section)

This information is processed only to provide and improve the Service.

2.5 Analytics Data

We may collect anonymized or aggregated data to analyze platform performance and usage trends.

3. How We Use Your Information

Flashloop may use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain the Service
  • Process subscriptions and payments
  • Generate AI outputs requested by users
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Detect fraud, abuse, or security risks
  • Monitor system performance
  • Provide customer support
  • Communicate service updates

4. Legal Basis for Processing (for applicable jurisdictions)

Depending on your location, we process personal information based on:

  • Performance of a contract (providing the Service)
  • Legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement)
  • Compliance with legal obligations
  • Your consent where required by law

5. Data Sharing

Flashloop does not sell personal information.

We may share information in limited circumstances, including:

Service Providers

  • Cloud infrastructure providers

6. AI Assistant Connections

You can connect Flashloop to an AI assistant that supports the Model Context Protocol (MCP), such as ChatGPT, Claude or Cursor. The assistant can then create and manage content in your Flashloop account for you. This section explains what happens to your data when you do.

6.1 Connecting and Disconnecting

To connect, you sign in to Flashloop and approve the connection. The assistant receives a connection key that lets it use Flashloop for you. We keep the Flashloop sign-in tokens behind that key on our connection service and renew them automatically, so you stay connected.

We delete a connection after 30 days without use. You can disconnect Flashloop at any time in your assistant's settings. If you delete your Flashloop account, its connections stop working.

6.2 What We Receive From the Assistant

We only receive what the assistant sends when it uses a Flashloop tool, for example:

  • Prompts and generation settings, such as the model, length, aspect ratio or voice
  • Images, videos or audio you ask it to use, as files or links
  • Text for voiceovers, and names or descriptions for saved characters, locations, elements and voices

We do not receive your conversation history.

6.3 What We Send Back to the Assistant

Depending on what you ask for, Flashloop tools return:

  • Your email address, credit balance and plan (tier, status and renewal date)
  • Links to the images, videos and audio you generate, and their progress
  • Your generation history, saved references and voices
  • Credit cost estimates and error messages

If your assistant account belongs to an organization, the assistant may also ask us for your email address and whether it is verified, so it can apply your organization's access rules.

6.4 The Assistant's Own Privacy Practices

The company that runs your assistant (for example OpenAI for ChatGPT, or Anthropic for Claude) handles your conversation and the results Flashloop returns under its own privacy policy and terms. Review them before you connect.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Flashloop may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Operate platform functionality
  • Remember user preferences
  • Analyze usage patterns
  • Improve performance

Users may manage cookie preferences through browser settings.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy:

  • Account information (name, email address, profile): while your account is active. It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Your library (prompts, uploads, generated images, videos and audio, saved references and cloned voices): until you delete an item or your account. When you delete a generated image or video, it leaves your library right away; we keep a hidden record of it (prompt, settings and media link) for fraud prevention, security and analytics.
  • AI assistant connections: deleted after 30 days without use (see the AI Assistant Connections section).
  • Payment records: kept by us and our payment providers for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

When you delete your account, we delete your account and sign-in information right away. Generated images and videos, with their prompts, and your credit and usage history are kept in de-identified form: we unlink them from you so they can no longer be tied to your account, and use them only for fraud prevention, security and analytics. If you have an active paid subscription, cancel it before deleting your account.

9. Data Security

Flashloop implements reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission
  • Secure cloud infrastructure
  • Access control systems
  • Monitoring for unauthorized activity

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

10. International Data Transfers

These countries may have different data protection laws.

By using the Service, you consent to such transfers where permitted by law.

16. The Super Agent

The Super Agent is Flashloop's AI assistant. It chats with you, generates images, video and voice, searches the web, works with items already in your account, and runs code in a sandbox to prepare files for you. It is an AI system, not a person. This section describes what it sends outside Flashloop; Information We Collect and Data Sharing above cover the rest.

16.1 Who Runs the Models

Flashloop does not call AI companies directly for the agent. Its chat, its web searches and one of its voice models run through OpenRouter, which routes each request to the company behind the model you choose: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, xAI, DeepSeek, Moonshot AI, Z.ai or ByteDance. Choosing a model decides which of them receives that conversation. OpenRouter describes its own handling of that data at https://openrouter.ai/privacy.

Three background tasks always use Google models through OpenRouter, whatever model you picked: naming a new conversation from its first message, compressing the notes the agent keeps for you, and describing or transcribing audio and video you attach to a Skill.

Images, video and voice are generated by the providers behind the model used for each generation: Google, OpenAI, xAI, Alibaba, ByteDance, Kling, MiniMax, ElevenLabs or Black Forest Labs, some of them reached through the routing services kie.ai or OpenRouter.

16.2 What the Agent Sends

Depending on what you ask it to do, running the agent can send outside Flashloop your messages to it, files and images you attach to the conversation, items from your library it works with, notes you ask it to remember, and the text of the web searches it runs for you.

16.3 Where It Goes and What Happens To It

The companies above are established outside many users' countries, including in the United States and in China, and their laws may protect personal data differently. International Data Transfers above applies to these transfers; to ask which protections apply to a specific provider, write to the address in Contact Information.

Each provider then handles that content under its own terms and privacy policy, not this one. Flashloop does not set any data-retention or model-training preference with OpenRouter.

16.4 Reports and Deletion

If you report a reply the agent gave you, Flashloop stores a copy of the reported text and of the model that produced it, so it can still be reviewed after the original message is gone. Deleting your account deletes that record too.

Deleting a conversation removes it from the app while your account exists. Deleting your account removes your agent conversations and their messages. Data Retention above covers everything else.
