What is GPT 5.6 Sol?

GPT 5.6 Sol is the flagship of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 series, generally available since July 9, 2026.

OpenAI reported state-of-the-art results at launch across coding, knowledge work, cybersecurity and science while using fewer tokens, and the model introduced the max reasoning effort. OpenAI also says it has stronger computer use and design judgment, checking and refining rendered results before it hands work back. Terra and Luna sit below it in the GPT-5.6 lineup, and the GPT-6 series has since followed.

What you can do with GPT 5.6 Sol

  • Top model of the GPT-5.6 series

  • First OpenAI model with a max reasoning effort

  • Inspects and refines rendered results before handing work back, according to OpenAI

  • Six reasoning levels, from off to max

  • Images and text in a 1.05M-token window

How to prompt GPT 5.6 Sol

  • Break technical work into clear steps and ask it to confirm each one.
  • Turn reasoning off for quick questions and raise it for hard ones.
  • If you liked its answers before GPT-6, keep using it; it is still in the featured list.

Where GPT 5.6 Sol fits best

  • Hard reasoning problems

  • Multi-step technical tasks

  • Long problem solving

  • Comparing results against the GPT-6 models

Questions about GPT 5.6 Sol

What is GPT 5.6 Sol?

The flagship of OpenAI's GPT-5.6 series, aimed at coding, knowledge work, cybersecurity and science. It was the first OpenAI model with the max effort setting.

How does GPT 5.6 Sol relate to GPT 5.6 Terra and Luna?

Sol is the top tier. Terra is the balanced middle model and Luna is the fast, low-cost one.

Should I use GPT 5.6 Sol or a GPT-6 model?

The GPT-6 models are OpenAI's newer generation. GPT 5.6 Sol is still in the featured section of the model picker, so you can compare both on your own tasks.

What reasoning levels does GPT 5.6 Sol support?

Off, low, medium, high, extra high and max. Super Agent starts it at low.
