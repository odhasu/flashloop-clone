What is Seedance 2.0 Mini?

Seedance 2.0 Mini is the lower-cost model in ByteDance's Seedance 2.0 family, trading some quality for price.

It keeps native audio and the reference and frame controls of Seedance 2.0 but stops at 720p. On Flashloop it renders clips up to 15 seconds, with up to 7 reference images or a first and last frame.

It exists for volume. It costs less per second than standard Seedance 2.0, so you can try several versions of a shot for the same budget. The prompts carry over, so a take you like on Mini can be re-rendered on Seedance 2.0 High at 1080p or 4K without rewriting anything.

For social clips that live at 720p on a phone screen, Mini is often the final model as well as the draft one. Where it falls short is resolution: anything headed for a large display, or anything you plan to crop, belongs on Seedance 2.0.

Questions about Seedance 2.0 Mini

How is Seedance 2.0 Mini different from Seedance 2.0?

Mini is the lower-cost model in the same family, trading some quality for price, and is capped at 480p and 720p. Seedance 2.0 adds 1080p and 4K on its High setting for the best fidelity.

What resolutions does Seedance 2.0 Mini support?

480p and 720p. For 1080p or 4K, use Seedance 2.0.

Does Seedance 2.0 Mini generate audio?

Yes. Audio and video are generated together, including ambience, music and voices.

What reference inputs can I use with Seedance 2.0 Mini on Flashloop?

Up to 7 reference images per generation, or a first and last frame instead. You cannot combine the two in one generation.

Who makes Seedance 2.0 Mini?

ByteDance's Seed team, as part of the Seedance 2.0 family. Flashloop makes it available next to the rest of its video model lineup.

Can I upgrade a Seedance 2.0 Mini clip to 4K?

Not the same render, but the same prompt. Run it on Seedance 2.0 with High selected and choose 1080p or 4K. The result will not be identical, but the prompt works the same way on both models.
