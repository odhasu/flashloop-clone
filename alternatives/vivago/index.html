By Victor Saisse
Founding Engineer at Flashloop

vivago.ai is cheap, broad and a little difficult to size up from the outside. We followed the model lists and the money to find alternatives that make sense for very different creators.

vivago.ai is a multi-model image and video studio from HiDream.ai. It mixes its own Vivago.ai 2.0 video model with Seedance 2.0, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 and Happy Horse, plus effect templates, lip sync, 4K upscaling and 3D tools. The homepage now opens vivago R1, a chat-based creative agent, while the older studio remains available. Two front doors, several model menus and a pricing page later, the product can be harder to understand than to open.

The reasons to leave are more concrete: long queues when servers are busy, credits reportedly taken for generations that never finish, no Seedance 2.5 or Wan 3.0 in the video menu, and no current iOS listing at the link on vivago.ai's own site. The reasons to stay are concrete too, especially the low yearly price, Veo 3.1 and the extra 3D and effects tools.

The bits that deserve a raised eyebrow

vivago.ai's Basic plan is $5 a month billed annually ($60 a year). Monthly billing begins with Plus at $20 a month for 4,000 credits. That makes the headline entry price true and slightly less simple than it first appears. These are the complaints that recur in public reviews.

What vivago.ai users complain about
  • Several Trustpilot reviewers describe long waits when servers are busy, including a video promised in 4 minutes that still hadn't arrived 3 to 5 hours later. Vivago's own FAQ says slow queues come from surging demand and suggests a membership for priority processing. (Sources: Trustpilot 1, Trustpilot 2, Trustpilot 3, Vivago FAQ)
  • Some users report credits charged for generations that never finish, saying credits are taken when they press Generate even if congestion or a glitch stops the result. Vivago's API pricing says failed generations don't use points, but its membership page and terms don't say the same about plan credits. (Sources: Trustpilot 1, Trustpilot 2, Vivago API pricing)
  • Free users also mention a lot of ads in the app, including ads when opening templates and when generating. Google Play marks the app as containing ads, and some reviewers accept them as the price of free credits. (Sources: Google Play reviews, Vivago plan comparison)
  • Trustpilot reviewers also say support replies are slow or missing, though one 2026 reviewer says emails were answered within 1 to 3 hours. (Sources: Trustpilot 1, Trustpilot 2, Trustpilot 3, Trustpilot 4)

Eighteen Trustpilot reviews are a thin sample, especially when at least two look off-topic. That profile scores 2.5. The Android app tells a very different story: 4.3 from about 171,000 ratings and 50M+ downloads. vivago.ai's pricing FAQ says digital services are generally non-refundable after activation, with a 48-hour window to contact support about technical errors or duplicate charges. Flashloop's own terms are also strict: purchases are final and non-refundable, and plan credits do not roll over.

How we checked a hard-to-read product

We build Flashloop and use its models every day, including Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 and Wan 3.0. This is competitor research, not an independent lab report.

Because vivago.ai's R1 homepage, older studio and app listings do not tell one neat story, we followed each claim to the live surface where it appears. In September 2026 we checked pricing pages, model menus, App Store and Google Play listings and public reviews for all seven products. We did not test them hands-on. The screenshots show homepages, and the summaries stick to verifiable models, free plans, starting prices, apps and the main compromise.

Where Flashloop is better

Flashloop is the cleaner pick for Seedance 2.5, Wan video and iPhone access. It also packages trend formats around reusable characters. Those are meaningful upgrades only if you use them; they do not magically make a $59 Creator plan cheaper than vivago.ai's $5 yearly entry.

Where vivago.ai is better

vivago.ai's Basic plan is $5 a month billed yearly, and new accounts get 30 free credits with no payment required. Its picker includes Veo 3.1 and its own Vivago.ai 2.0 model, which Flashloop does not. The same account also has effect templates, lip sync with preset voices, 4K image and video upscaling, background removal, text or image to 3D, and the vivago R1 chat agent. If those are why you use it, staying there makes sense.

My verdict

I would start with Flashloop when Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0 and an iOS app are the missing pieces. Kling 3.0 and Seedance 2.0 remain available, and another model can take over when a shot goes sideways.

I would stay with vivago.ai for Veo 3.1, 3D generation or the $5 yearly plan. For a cheaper multi-model phone app, Pollo AI deserves a look before the more expensive options.

Sale prices are not a personality

The $5 figure is real, but it buys a year in one transaction. Start there, then work outward from what you actually generate.

  1. Count usable outputs. A clip that needs several attempts, or a job that never finishes after taking credits, costs more than one generation in practice.
  2. Match each model to its plan. Every vivago.ai paid plan lists access to all AI tools. Flashloop puts Kling 3.0 and Seedance 2.5 on Creator, not Starter.
  3. Separate yearly pricing from monthly pricing. vivago.ai's $5 Basic is yearly only; month-to-month billing begins with Plus at $20. Pollo AI labels its yearly Pro price as a flash sale.
  4. Price the missing feature rather than the whole replacement. If you need Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0 or an iOS app that vivago.ai does not currently list, one multi-model plan may cost less than stacking a second service.

vivago.ai's pricing and promotions change, so check its current pricing page before deciding.

  • Choose Flashloop if you want Seedance 2.5, Kling, Veo and Wan, plus both phone apps.
  • Choose Kling AI if Kling is the model you actually want, from its maker.
  • Choose Higgsfield if you want cinematic presets and don't need a mobile app.
  • Choose Krea if you want to experiment, compare models and build visual workflows.
  • Choose Pollo AI if you want a cheaper multi-model picker with iOS and Android.
  • Choose DeeVid if templates and avatars around Seedance and Kling are the job.
  • Choose ImagineArt if image models and a large app library matter more than price.

Keep vivago.ai if the $5 yearly Basic plan, 30 free credits, Veo 3.1, effect templates or 3D tools are the point. A newer Seedance version is not much of an upgrade if it costs you the tools you open every day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best vivago.ai alternative?

If newer named models and reliable phone access are the priority, our pick is Flashloop. It combines Seedance 2.5, Kling 3.0, Veo 3.1 and Wan 3.0 with character and trend workflows on web, iOS and Android.

Kling AI is the better fit if Kling is the only model you use. Pollo AI and DeeVid are closer if you want another multi-model picker with both iOS and Android apps.

Is Flashloop better than vivago.ai?

It depends on the workflow.

Flashloop is a better fit if you want Seedance 2.5, Wan 3.0 and MiniMax video models, plus an iOS app. vivago.ai is a better fit if you want a $5 yearly Basic plan, 30 free sign-up credits, its own Vivago.ai 2.0 model, effect templates or 3D generation.

Does vivago.ai have Seedance 2.5?

No. vivago.ai's live video picker lists Seedance 2.0, not Seedance 2.5. Flashloop, Higgsfield, Krea, Pollo AI, DeeVid and ImagineArt all list Seedance 2.5. On Flashloop, Seedance 2.5 starts on the Creator plan at $59 a month, or $47 a month billed annually.

What is the best free vivago.ai alternative?

Krea's free plan gives you 100 compute units a day with no credit card, though free video is limited to 2 LTX-2 clips a day. Kling AI has a free plan with watermarked videos, Pollo AI starts with 20 credits, and DeeVid gives 20 signup credits with watermarks. ImagineArt gives 100 credits a day, but video generation needs a paid plan. Flashloop has no free plan.

vivago.ai mixes signup credits, annual discounts and changing model access. Treat every free allowance as a live detail to verify, not a feature to engrave in a comparison chart.

Which vivago.ai alternative is cheaper?

There isn't a universal answer. vivago.ai's cheapest paid plan is Basic at $5 a month billed annually ($60 a year). On monthly billing, Plus starts at $20 a month. Among the alternatives, Kling AI's Standard plan is $6.99 for the first month, then $8.80 a month, Krea Basic is $9 a month, ImagineArt Basic is $13 a month, DeeVid Lite is $14 a month and Pollo AI Lite is $15 a month.

Cheaper plans often leave out the newest models or cap resolution, so compare the cost of the model, duration and resolution you actually use.

Does vivago.ai have a mobile app?

Android only. The Google Play listing is vivago.ai - AI Image & Video from Sparking Innovations Limited. The iOS app its site still links to is not on the App Store. Among the alternatives, Flashloop, Kling AI, Pollo AI, DeeVid and ImagineArt have iOS and Android apps, Krea has an iOS app, and Higgsfield has no official app.

Where else can I use Kling 3.0 and Veo 3.1?

vivago.ai lists both Kling 3.0 and Veo 3.1 in its video picker. Kling 3.0 is also on Flashloop, Higgsfield, Krea, Pollo AI, DeeVid and ImagineArt, and on Kling AI itself. Veo 3.1 is on Flashloop, Higgsfield, Krea, Pollo AI, DeeVid and ImagineArt.
